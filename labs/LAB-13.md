# LAB 13 — Authorization Check

## Objective
Determine whether the administrative action is documented as authorized.

## Evidence
Change-control and audit records.

## Task
Classify the action as AUTHORIZED, UNAUTHORIZED, or UNKNOWN.

## Answer Format
`AUTHORIZED` / `UNAUTHORIZED` / `UNKNOWN`

## Scope Boundary
Absence of a change ticket is not automatically proof of malicious intent.
