# Scope and Investigator Boundaries

## Authorized Scope

The analyst may examine, parse, correlate, hash, timeline and report on the fictional artifacts included with this case.

## Prohibited Actions

The analyst must not:
- intercept or record real communications;
- monitor live calls;
- access real carrier, PBX, voicemail or telephone accounts;
- obtain third-party records;
- bypass credentials or authentication;
- scan or probe external systems;
- contact fictional subjects as if they were real;
- attempt attribution beyond the supplied evidence.

## Evidentiary Standard

Audio interference, echo, static, dropped calls, user suspicion, or an unusual routing event alone is not proof of interception.

Findings must distinguish:
- **FACT** — directly supported by evidence;
- **INFERENCE** — reasoned interpretation supported by evidence;
- **UNKNOWN** — not established by the available evidence.

When evidence cannot establish a claim, the correct forensic response is to state the limitation.
