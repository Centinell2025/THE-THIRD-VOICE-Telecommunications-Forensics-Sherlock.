# Case Scenario

A fictional enterprise receives multiple reports of abnormal behavior during confidential business calls. Users describe intermittent audio anomalies and unexpected connection behavior. Internal support initially attributes the reports to ordinary network instability.

A later audit identifies inconsistencies between call-detail records, SIP signaling, PBX routing events, authentication activity and administrative changes.

The forensic examiner is provided a preserved evidence package. The examiner is not asked to conduct surveillance. The assignment is retrospective: reconstruct what occurred, identify which anomalies have benign explanations, determine which events require escalation, and establish whether the evidence supports unauthorized interception.

All organizations, people, numbers, addresses, systems and communications in this case are fictional.
